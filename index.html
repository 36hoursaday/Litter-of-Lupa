    Highway RPG Rules (3-Stat Lite)

    Zero math. When an action has danger, tap the d20 button and apply your Stat bonus (+0, +1, +2, or higher with Level Up).

    1 – 9: HARD COMPLICATION

    Failure. Suffer 1 Heart of damage, trigger an alarm, or lose tactical ground.

    10 – 14: SUCCESS AT A COST

    Action succeeds, but suffer a twist: 1 Heart taken, broken gear, or loud noise alerted nearby enemies.

    15+: CLEAN SUCCESS

    Total success. You overcome the obstacle without suffering counter-damage.

    The Werewolf Curse

    • • Beast Marks (Focus): Spend 1 mark to activate wolf blood powers. They regenerate during camp rests.
    • • 0 Hearts = Wild Wolf Frenzy: If reduced to 0 Hearts, you do not perish—you shift into an uncontrollable feral predator. Sibling players must physically grapple or soothe you.
    • • Silver Vulnerability: Silver bullets or blades deal double damage (2 Hearts) and the wound cannot be rested away until treated with alchemical salve.

    Adversary Quick Reference

    Common Minion (1 Heart): Watchmen, sailors, thugs. Defeated in 1 successful strike.
    Inquisitor / Elite (2 Hearts): Carries silver rapier or blessed ammunition. Has 1 unique trick (flash powder, wolfsbane).
    Behemoth / Automaton (4–5 Hearts): Lycan’s experimental clockwork creations or feral monsters. Takes 2 actions per round.

    Core Lore

    Professor Lycan: Frail, ancient human scholar kept alive by an imperfect Philosopher's Stone. He seeks Lupa to claim the Fountain of Youth water to restore his youth.

    Lupa (Mother): The legendary ancient she-wolf who suckled Romulus & Remus. She drank half a vial centuries ago, keeping her physically 40 years old despite being 2,500 years old. She separated her children across Europe and the colonies to protect them from Lycan's greed.
